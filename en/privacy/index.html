Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Summary

  • You can install Gorani and use its basic features without an account. 3D and 2D result files stay on your computer and are never sent to Gorani servers.
  • For members (Google sign-in) we use only your Google account ID and email address. The name and photo that Google sign-in also passes are stored by the sign-in service (Firebase) but not used. We never receive your password.
  • Membership is free. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
  • If you delete your account, your member data and generation history are deleted immediately.

This policy explains how the operators of Gorani (“we”) process personal data, as required by Article 30 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). It covers the Gorani website (gorani.site), the Gorani plugin (Rhino, SketchUp, Blender, Archicad, AutoCAD) and member features. This English version is a translation; if it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version applies.

Plugin usage statistics never include names, email addresses, file paths, search terms or drawing content. The Blender, Archicad and AutoCAD versions send no usage statistics, and the SketchUp version sends them only if you choose [Send] when first asked. The Rhino version currently has no off switch. If you object, email us your installation ID (the value in ~/.gorani/client_id) and we will delete the records collected under it.

When you sign in, the plugin stores the sign-in key, email and entitlements in ~/.gorani/account.json on your computer. Signing out deletes the sign-in key.

2. Google account data

Google sign-in (the Sign in with Google button) passes your Google account ID and email address together with your name and profile photo. Gorani uses only the account ID and email; the name and photo are stored by the sign-in service (Firebase) but not used. We use this data only for the purposes in section 1, never share or sell them, and never use them for advertising. Gorani’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Children

You must be at least 14 years old to become a member. Using the basic features without an account is not restricted. If we learn that a child under 14 has signed up, we delete the account.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal data with anyone, except when required by law through due process, for example by law enforcement.

If you do not want your data transferred abroad, do not sign up or use the contact form, or delete your account. You can refuse website analytics as described in section 7. You can still install Gorani and use the basic features in every case.

6. Deletion

We delete data within 5 days after the retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled. Member data and generation history are deleted from Firebase immediately, leaving no copies. For contact messages we delete the spreadsheet rows and emails, and clean up copies left in Google Sheets version history every quarter. If another law requires us to keep data, we keep it separately only for that period (none at present).

7. Cookies and browser storage

  • The website uses Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) for visit statistics. You can refuse them by blocking cookies in your browser or using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on; the website works the same either way.
  • Your language and subtitle choices and the latest version information are kept only in your browser storage and are not sent to our servers. Your website sign-in key stays in browser storage until you sign out and is sent only to the sign-in service (Firebase).
  • We do not run targeted advertising and do not use your activity for ads.

8. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your personal data or to stop its processing, and you can withdraw consent (PIPA Art. 35–37). On the website account page (gorani.site/account) you can delete your account, delete your generation history and disconnect programs yourself. Requests by email (gorani.3d@gmail.com) are handled within 10 days. You may also act through a legal representative or an authorized person. If we must refuse a request under the law, we will tell you why. If you live in the EU or UK, you also have the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security measures

  • Only the two operators’ accounts can open member data (Firebase console) and the contact spreadsheet, and the operating accounts use two-step verification.
  • Security rules let only you read and write your generation history, connected programs and settings. When the browser passes the sign-in key to the plugin, it goes only to this computer (127.0.0.1) in a form post, so it is not left in the address bar or browsing history.
  • Member data and contact messages are sent over HTTPS.
  • We use only what is necessary. We never receive passwords.

12. Services your computer contacts directly

The plugin connects directly from your computer to the services below. Each service processes your IP address and other data under its own policy. Gorani does not receive those records.

  • OpenStreetMap Overpass and Nominatim (data for sites outside Korea, place search), OpenStreetMap map tiles, OpenFreeMap (the same OSM data when Overpass is unavailable)
  • AWS Terrain Tiles, Mapterhorn (high-resolution terrain outside Korea), GSI (Japan) elevation tiles (terrain)
  • VWorld (Korean Ministry of Land) map and aerial tiles, Esri aerial imagery (outside Korea), VWorld address search (only if you enter your own key)
  • ipinfo.io — contacted once when the panel opens to choose the first map position (not in the Blender version). It guesses your city from your IP address; nothing is stored.
  • Google sign-in — sign-in happens between your browser and Google; Gorani never receives your password.

13. Changes to this policy

We announce changes on the website at least 7 days before they take effect, or 30 days for changes that are unfavorable to you or significant, both on the website and in the Gorani panel. Earlier versions are in the change history.
